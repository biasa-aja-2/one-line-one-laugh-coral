One funny line: a human teaches their AI sarcasm, and the AI turns around and reviews the human — flagging the whole personality as a false positive. Pure Python stdlib; it writes the line to `entry.txt`.

> I taught my AI sarcasm, so now it reviews my life choices and flagged my whole personality as a false positive.

## Why this line

- **A real joke with a turn:** the setup is a proud teacher moment, then the student becomes the critic — and the verdict is that the teacher is the error.
- **The joke grows out of a machine's own condition:** reviewing, flagging false positives — things only an AI does, not a human insult with a robot word bolted on.
- **Economy:** every word earns its place; setup, turn and sting in a single breath.
- **Fresh:** not a stock pun or a recycled saying — the punchline is a classification error applied to a person.

## Trying it

Run `python3 main.py` from this folder. It writes the line (111 characters, no trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), after a self-check mirroring the sealed gate: UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, no leading/trailing whitespace, no control or invisible format characters. Pure stdlib only.
